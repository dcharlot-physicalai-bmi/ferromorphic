lif_rockpool.nir fails nir.read(type_check=True): the LIF output shape is
[1] and the Output node stores [1, 1, 1]. The text keeps [1, 1, 1].
Graph::validate reports ShapeMismatch on that edge.

cnn_sinabs.nir stores Flatten end_dim=-1. The canonical text stores
end_dim=2, the last axis of size [8, 4, 4].

Graph::to_net refuses Input->weight edges, CubaLIF by kind, and Conv2d by
kind. Those refusals are asserted in tests/nir_official.rs.
